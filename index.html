Hi, I’m Evan.

I’ve designed and built websites for eleven years, first inside studios and on my own since 2019. Working solo means you talk to the person doing the work, every time.

I care about the unglamorous parts: how fast a page loads on a weak signal, whether a form makes sense to someone in a hurry, whether the words sound like you. Those details are usually the difference between a site people admire and a site that brings in work.

No account managers, no handoffs, no surprises on the invoice.
